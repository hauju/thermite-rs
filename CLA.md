## How to sign

Include the following line in the description of your first pull request, with your own name and
email:

```
I have read and agree to the Thermite CLA (CLA.md).
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email.example>
```

That signature covers that pull request and every later contribution you make to the Project. You do
not need to repeat it.

**Contributing on behalf of a company?** If your employer owns the rights to your work, a corporate
agreement is needed instead. Email <mail@haukejung.de> before opening the pull request.
