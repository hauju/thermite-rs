## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.
They help websites function properly, enhance user experience, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies may be:
- **Session cookies** – deleted automatically when you close your browser.
- **Persistent cookies** – stored until they expire or are deleted manually.
- **First-party cookies** – set by our website.
- **Third-party cookies** – set by external services integrated into our website.

## 3. What We Actually Store

| Name | Type | Purpose | Consent Required | Lifetime |
|------|------|---------|------------------|----------|
| **Session cookie** | Strictly necessary, first-party, signed | Keeps you signed in and links your browser to your server-side session. Requests that change state are additionally protected by an origin check (CSRF protection). | No | Expires after 7 days of inactivity |
| **`theme`** | Not a cookie — browser local storage | Remembers your light/dark preference so the page does not flash the wrong theme on load. Read and written entirely in your browser. | No | Until you clear site data |

The `theme` value is listed for completeness because §25 TDDDG covers storage on your device generally, not only cookies. It is **never transmitted to our servers** and contains nothing but the strings `light` or `dark`.

**We set no analytics, advertising, personalization or marketing cookies of any kind**, and we embed no third-party scripts that could set one.

## 5. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
Disabling our **session cookie** will make it impossible to sign in — it is the only thing keeping you logged in. Public pages, including the documentation, work fine without it.

Here are links to cookie management instructions for popular browsers:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
- [Safari (macOS/iOS)](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac)

You can also sign out from the dashboard, which invalidates the session and clears the cookie.

## 6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & TDDDG)

Under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and **§25 of the German TDDDG** (which replaced the TTDSG), we may store or access information on your device only if:

- It is **strictly necessary** to provide the service you requested; or
- You have **given your prior consent**.

Everything described in Section 3 falls under the first case: the session cookie is required to deliver an authenticated service you asked for, and the theme preference is required to render the interface you asked for. The legal basis for the associated personal data processing is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — necessary to perform the service.

We currently rely on **no consent-based storage at all**, so there is nothing for you to withdraw.
